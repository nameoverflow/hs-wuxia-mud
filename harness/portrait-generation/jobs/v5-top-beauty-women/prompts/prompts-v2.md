Common hard fixes from review:

- Upper body must be much smaller: head and hair occupy about 78-85% of image height; clothing is only neck, collar, and shoulder tips in the lower 12-18%.
- No arms, hands, waist, chest-heavy bust, or large torso.
- Three hairstyles must be visibly different; do not use the same single tied-back hair.
- Clothing must be characterful even though only collar and shoulder tips are visible.
- Keep the rough v4 parchment ink style, not polished anime.
