# v5 Top Beauty Women Portrait Prompts - Rerun v3

Common correction:

- Tight face-and-collar avatar, not a bust illustration.
- Face and hair should fill 85-90% of the square.
- Clothing is only a narrow collar strip at the bottom 5-10% plus tiny shoulder hints.
- No visible chest, robe front panels, torso mass, arms, hands, or waist.
- Hair and hair ornaments must be distinct across the three portraits.

## 01 Seductive Contempt

Create one original square Jianghu female character portrait in the locked v4 rough parchment style.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-size source image for a wuxia MUD character portrait candidate
Character target: top-tier beauty, 妖艳魅惑风, dangerous heretical Jianghu woman
Style: old Chinese mobile wuxia portrait, rough thick uneven black ink brush lines, dry-brush grain, muted flat watercolor on aged tan rice paper, controlled low-to-moderate detail, not polished anime
STRICT COMPOSITION: this is a tight face-and-collar avatar, NOT a bust illustration. The face and hair must fill 85-90% of the square. The image ends immediately below the robe collar. Clothing is only a very narrow collar strip at the bottom 5-10% plus tiny shoulder tips; no visible chest, no robe front panels, no torso mass, no arms, no hands. Keep full hair and chin visible.
Pose/angle: frontal face, chin lifted and head tilted back slightly, eyelids lowered, looking down at the viewer with a superior gaze, light contemptuous smile; not a side profile and not a 45-degree half-profile
Face: top-tier seductive beauty through sharp oval face, narrow fox-like eyes, arched brows, high cheekbones, elegant lips curled in a contemptuous smile
Hair: elaborate high coiled updo with side loops, slanting dark hairpin, tassel ornament, dramatic but rough ink; clearly not a single ponytail or simple bun
Clothing visible only at bottom: flamboyant dark wine-red and charcoal asymmetrical high collar, black gauze scarf edge, tiny muted metal ornament; clothing should feel bold even though only a narrow collar strip is visible
Constraints: square parchment portrait, complete original image, no text, no logo, no watermark, no transparent background
Avoid: large upper body, chest-heavy bust, visible torso, robe front panels below collar, arms, hands, waist, plain clothing, repeated single tied-back hair, polished anime, photorealism, 3D render, thin pencil lines, excessive hair strands, fantasy armor, complex scenery, UI border
